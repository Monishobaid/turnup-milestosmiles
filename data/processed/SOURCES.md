# TURNUP connected-data provenance

The processed files were generated by `scripts/audit_data.py` from these public sources:

- Hack the Climate: <https://hacktheclimate.io/> → `generation-load-prices.zip`.
- EirGrid: <https://www.eirgrid.ie/grid/system-and-renewable-data-reports> → `DD-HH-2025-v10.xlsx` and `DD-HH-2026-V9.xlsx`.

Only `IE_generation.csv`, `IE_load.csv`, and `IE_SEM_prices.csv` are unpacked from the Hack the Climate archive. EirGrid rows are restricted to `UT_TYPE=Wind` and `JURISDICTION=IE`. EirGrid local timestamps are converted to UTC using `GMT_OFFSET` before joining.

The join keeps `system_curtailment_mwh` (`CURTAILMENTS_MWH`) and `local_constraint_mwh` (`CONSTRAINTS_MWH`) separate. The EirGrid `DD_MWH` source total is preserved as `total_dispatch_down_mwh`.
